# Fonts free for commercial use

Licences that allow commercial use on websites, apps and printed material without a purchase:

| Licence | Short | Commercial web use | Self-hosting / bundling | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SIL Open Font License 1.1 | OFL | yes | yes | may not be sold on its own; renamed derivatives need a new name; keep the `OFL.txt` next to bundled files |
| Apache License 2.0 | Apache | yes | yes | attribution in the licence file, nothing on the page |
| Ubuntu Font Licence 1.0 | UFL | yes | yes | Ubuntu family only |
| Bitstream Vera / DejaVu licence | Vera | yes | yes | DejaVu family |

**Rule: any family served by `fonts.googleapis.com` counts as free.** Google Fonts only hosts
OFL, Apache and UFL families. A family from Google Fonts that is self-hosted (`@fontsource/*`,
downloaded `.woff2`) is still free under the same licence.
